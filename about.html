Introduction

The Coding Awareness Program for School Children is a Community Engagement Program designed to introduce students to basic programming concepts. It encourages children to explore technology and develop logical thinking through simple activities.

Objectives of the Program

  • Introduce students to basic coding concepts.
  • Develop logical thinking and problem-solving skills.
  • Encourage creativity and innovation.
  • Improve awareness of computers and technology.
  • Promote safe and responsible digital practices.

Target Audience

The program is intended for school children. Activities can be adjusted according to the students' age, class and previous knowledge.

Importance of Coding

Coding helps students understand how digital applications work. It can also develop patience, logical reasoning, creativity and teamwork.
